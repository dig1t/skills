## Monetization Insights

### Platform Revenue Context
- Developer payouts: $1.5B in 2025 (+70% YoY in Q4)
- Top 1,000 creators average $1.3M each
- 36.7M monthly paying users (+94% YoY)
- Avg spend per payer: $20.18 (+53% YoY)

### What's Working:
- **Simulators** - Most profitable genre (starter packs, VIP, events)
- **Subscriptions** - No platform fee on renewals (better rev share)
- **Avatar cosmetics** - Strongest spending driver (identity > power)
- **Limited virtual goods** - Scarcity drives discovery spikes
- **Event-based releases** - FOMO-driven, 60% of revenue for some games
- **Regional pricing** - Improves international conversion
- **Ethical monetization** - Cosmetics for self-expression > pay-to-win

### New Patterns:
- In-experience subscriptions (better revenue split)
- Rewarded video ads expanding (Amazon DSP, Liftoff, Index Exchange) — but note: **ad revenue does NOT feed the Home recommendation algorithm**, only IAP Robux spend does. Use the "reduce ads to likely payers" toggle to avoid IAP cannibalization.
- **RFY ranker now measures a 28-day window (live June 15, 2026):** Roblox expanded the Recommended For You ranker's measurement window from 7 days to 28 days to reward long-term retention and monetization over short-term hooks. Retention-style signals (playtime, play days, qualified play sessions, intentional co-play days, spend days, Robux spent) are now reported as three separate averages: **Day 1**, **Day 2-7**, and **Day 8-28**. Implication for ideation: concepts that retain and re-monetize players across the full first month now beat first-session/first-week hooks. (Webinar June 17, 2026 11am PST; docs https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery; overview video https://youtu.be/K7jsk3bzmvE.)
- **RFY now publishes RELATIVE signal importance** (still no exact numeric weights; weighting stays dynamic/personalized). *Most Important:* Play Through Rate (PTR — replaces the retired QPTR), First Play Bounce Rate (NEGATIVE signal, penalized at **under 60s** and **61-180s**), Play Days Per User, Playtime Per User (**capped at 60 min/user/game/day**). *Important:* Intentional Co-Play Days Per User, Qualified Play Sessions Per User, Spend Days Per User, Robux Spent Per User. This sharpens the "short-session formats outperform" trend note above: First Play Bounce Rate now punishes sub-60s/sub-180s drop-offs, so a short core loop still needs an immediate hook that survives the first minutes.
- **All RFY ranking signals count ONLY users who organically joined from the RFY sort on Home** (two-stage pipeline reaffirmed). Ads, curation, friends, search, and social can feed *retrieval* (get a game considered) but cannot buy *rank* — relevant when weighing the Ads Manager Earnings-objective note below: paid traffic drives retrieval and conversions but does not pollute RFY ranking signals.
- 1-Robux entry point model (low barrier, high volume)
- Starter packs at 99-199 Robux overcoming first-purchase barrier
- **US 18+ DevEx +42% bonus (effective June 8, 2026):** spend by age-checked 18+ US players in eligible games earns $0.54 per 100 Robux instead of $0.38. **Only GamePasses, DevProducts, in-game subscriptions, and private servers qualify — avatar items do NOT.** Requires R15 avatars. This restructures the math for any game targeting the 18-34 US cohort (which is growing 50% YoY and monetizes 50% higher than under-18). Implication for ideation: older-audience concepts that monetize via GamePasses/DevProducts beat concepts that monetize via Marketplace/avatar items by 42% at DevEx.
- **Earnings campaign objective (Ads Manager, May 2026):** eligibility-gated by ARPU threshold. Optimizes for users likely to *spend*. Higher CPP than Maximize Plays but ROAS-positive within 30 days for most early campaigns. Game ideas should consider whether they're designed to monetize well enough to pass the eligibility threshold.

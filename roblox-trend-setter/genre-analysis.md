## Underserved Niches (High Opportunity)

### 1. Sports Reimagination (FFA/Competitive)
**Current State:** Blade Ball is the only breakout. Basketball Zero and Blue Lock: Rivals emerging.
**Opportunity:** Archery, volleyball, sumo, fencing, boxing -- all untapped as FFA games
**Why Now:** Blade Ball proved the formula. Zero competition in most sports. 14M+ unmet searches.
**Dev Effort:** Low-Medium (simple arenas, focused mechanics)

### 2. Tycoon-Combat Hybrids
**Current State:** The Forge pioneering at 300K+ CCU
**Opportunity:** Forge + other themes (medieval, sci-fi, cooking + food fight)
**Why Now:** New genre fusion with proven demand. First movers win.

### 3. Music/Rhythm Games
**Current State:** Very few quality options on Roblox
**Opportunity:** Beat games, DJ simulators, band formation
**Why Now:** Music is universal, near-zero competition

### 4. Collection Sims (Non-Fishing)
**Current State:** Fish It! dominates fishing. Other collection themes wide open.
**Opportunity:** Fossils, gems, bugs, birds, cards, artifacts
**Why Now:** Fish It! proved the collection + progression formula at 900K CCU

### 5. Cooperative Experiences
**Current State:** Obby Puzzles trending, but cooperative games underserved
**Opportunity:** Co-op puzzles, team survival, cooperative building
**Why Now:** Social mechanics underserved vs competitive. Localization-friendly.

### 6. Narrative/Story Games
**Current State:** Very rare on Roblox
**Opportunity:** Choice-driven stories, visual novels, mystery adventures
**Why Now:** 18+ cohort growing fastest, more sophisticated content welcome. The June 15 2026 RFY update (now live) expanded the ranker's measurement window from 7 days to 28 days, reported as Day 1 / Day 2-7 / Day 8-28 averages — directly rewarding long-term retention (Day 8-28) via **Play Days Per User** and **Playtime Per User**, the natural strength of story-driven games. Click-quality is now judged by **Play Through Rate (PTR)** as the headline metric, while short-session quality is split out into a separate **First Play Bounce Rate** (a negative signal measured at under 60s and 61-180s) — so deep, slow-burn genres are no longer judged on a single short-funnel click metric the way the retired QPTR did. Genre viability now hinges on long-term retention (Day 8-28) plus monetization depth (**Spend Days Per User**, **Robux Spent Per User** across the 28-day window) driving distribution — not just first-session or first-week hooks — which favors genres that retain and monetize over the full first month (Roleplay, Collection Sims, Cooperative, Narrative). See https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery.

### Cross-cutting opportunity: Standout Games + 18+ DevEx alignment

Two platform shifts that compound when an idea hits both:

1. **Standout Games sort** (Mar 2026, curated by Roblox) actively rewards **novelty** — new mechanics, distinctive visuals, underrepresented genres. Submit via Curation team nomination form. Requires authentic gameplay video on details page (Apr 2026).
2. **US 18+ DevEx +42% bonus** (June 8, 2026) for age-checked 18+ US spend in R15 games on GamePasses/DevProducts/subscriptions/private servers (NOT avatar items).

The June 15 2026 RFY update compounds here too: its 28-day monetization signals (Spend Days Per User, Robux Spent Per User) reward IAP-driven depth across the full first month, stacking with the 18+ DevEx +42% bonus for sustained 18+ US spend.

Concepts that hit *both* — novel enough for Standout consideration AND designed for the 18+ US audience with R15 avatars and IAP-driven monetization — get a curation boost AND +42% effective revenue. Examples in the underserved niches above:

- **Narrative/Story games** for older audiences (18+ US, R15, IAP-monetized) — checks all boxes.
- **Sport Reimagination** with realistic R15 articulation and serious-tone aesthetics aimed at older players.
- **Cooperative Experiences** with deep social mechanics that retain mature players.

The *avoid* shape: an avatar-item-monetized casual game targeting under-13s with a R6 stylized look. Multiple structural disadvantages stacked.

# UGC / Virtual Merch Guide for Roblox

## Overview

UGC (User-Generated Content) on Roblox refers to avatar items that players can attach to their avatars. These are a major part of the Roblox ecosystem and can help promote games and initiatives with the right strategy.

> Note: Roblox is renaming these to "Virtual Merch"

## Pricing

*Rates are at cost in USD. Add profit margins or media fees separately.*

### Paid UGC
- **Upload cost:** ~$225 USD
- **Player pricing:** Set any Robux price
- **Quantity:** Unlimited OR limited edition
- **Limited drops:** Can create artificial scarcity

### Free UGC
- **Base cost:** $10 (one-time publishing fee)
- **Per unit cost:** $1 per unit
- **Example:** 3,000 free UGC units = $3,010 USD

### Cost Calculator
```
Paid UGC: $225 per item
Free UGC: $10 + ($1 × number_of_units)
```

## Requirements

### Account Requirements
- Uploader must have Premium 1000/2200 subscription ($10-20/month)
- Publisher must have Premium 1000/2200 subscription
- Uploader and publisher can be the same account

### Content Requirements
- Cannot copy pre-existing Roblox items
- Cannot intentionally disrupt gameplay (UI obstruction, etc.)
- Must be categorized as correct accessory type
- No real-world weaponry or violence

### Technical Requirements
| Spec | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Triangle count | Under 4,000 tris |
| Vertex count | Less than 1,200 vertices |
| Texture size | Up to 1024x1024 |

## Best Practices

### Challenge Design
When giving out free UGC, challenges should:
- Be appropriate difficulty for target audience
- Challenging enough for new AND existing players
- Achievable within reasonable playtime
- Clearly communicated to players

### Timing
- UGC tied to a game auto-links the acquisition location
- New players will come expecting to find the UGC
- Ensure UGC is available immediately after upload
- Delays risk losing the player influx

### Design Approach
- "Quirky = better" isn't always true
- Understand what your audience wants
- Research best-selling items from past week
- Design items relevant to your game category
- Balance uniqueness with desirability

### Limited Editions
- Lower quantities create higher demand
- Five-figure quantities (or less) work well
- Artificial scarcity drives engagement
- Only do limited editions if item is desirable

## UGC Strategy for Games

### Distribution Methods

| Method | Best For | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Achievement unlock** | Engagement | Requires completing challenge |
| **Login reward** | Retention | Daily/streak rewards |
| **Event participation** | Event engagement | Time-limited availability |
| **Purchase bonus** | Monetization | Bundled with Robux purchase |
| **Code redemption** | Physical products | Links to merchandise |
| **Contest prize** | Marketing | Competition winners |

### Recommended UGC Calendar

| Frequency | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| Weekly | 1 free UGC item (rotates character/theme) |
| Monthly | 1-2 event-specific items |
| Seasonal | 3-5 holiday/event items |
| Campaign | 4+ items for influencer/marketing campaigns |

### Character-Based UGC Strategy

For games with hero characters:
- Each character gets signature items
- Rotate through characters weekly
- Build complete "sets" over time
- Use character launches for new UGC drops

Example schedule:
```
Week 1: [Character A]'s Hair
Week 2: [Character B]'s Backpack
Week 3: [Character C]'s Glasses
Week 4: [Character D]'s Wings
Week 5: [Character A]'s Hat
...continue rotation
```

## UGC for Marketing Campaigns

### Influencer Campaigns
- Create co-branded items with creator input
- Team-specific items for competition campaigns
- Clear branding guidelines for approval
- Distribute to top performers

### Launch Campaigns
- Exclusive launch week items
- "Early adopter" badges/items
- Limited edition launch cosmetics

### Seasonal Events
- Halloween: Spooky/costume themed
- Winter: Holiday themed
- Summer: Beach/vacation themed
- Create urgency with time-limited availability

## Common Pitfalls

### Avoid These Mistakes
1. **Overpricing free UGC challenges** - Too hard = low claims
2. **Underestimating demand** - Running out of limited items
3. **Poor timing** - UGC not ready when announced
4. **Generic designs** - Items that don't stand out
5. **Ignoring mobile** - Items that don't look good on mobile
6. **No promotion** - Great UGC with no visibility

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Item looks good on all body types
- [ ] Item displays correctly on mobile
- [ ] Name and description are clear
- [ ] Tags are comprehensive for search
- [ ] Challenge/acquisition is fair and fun
- [ ] Promotion plan is in place
- [ ] Support ready for player questions
